Built to screen on merit, not on skim-speed.

TugmaSycan reads resumes the way a thorough reviewer would — if that reviewer never got tired, never skimmed, and could check every candidate against the exact same criteria. Built for recruiters, student organization leaders, and project managers who need to screen high volumes of applicants without losing track of why each one ranks where they do.

The problem it responds to

Hundreds of applicants, one role

Manually reading and comparing every resume for a single opening is rarely realistic once volume climbs.

Skimming misses people

Under time pressure, reviewers default to quick scans — and quick scans overlook qualified candidates.

Bias creeps in quietly

School name, address, or how a name reads can unintentionally outweigh actual skills and experience.

What it does

  • Upload resumes in bulk, in standard PDF or Word format
  • Automatic extraction of skills, education, certifications, and experience
  • Define the exact requirements a role needs, and score candidates against them
  • A full breakdown per candidate — what was met, partially met, or missing, not just a number
  • An anonymized Fair Review mode that hides identifying details during evaluation
  • Saved search history, so a screening session can be revisited later

Principles

Transparency over black-box scoring

Every ranking comes with a reason, not just a percentage.

Honest about its limits

Rule-based matching, not machine-learning prediction. No bias-audit model. Standard-format resumes only.

Decision support, not decision-making

TugmaSycan ranks and organizes candidates. The reviewer still decides.
